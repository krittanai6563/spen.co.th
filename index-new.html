About Us

Single Point
Engineering Co., Ltd.

Leader in quality engineering, high-precision products, modern inspection technology. 30+ years experience trusted by global partners.

For over 28 years, Single Point Engineering (SPEN) has been a trusted partner in high-precision manufacturing. As a BOI-promoted company with two state-of-the-art facilities in Thailand, SPEN is built on a legacy of excellence, continuous expansion, and technical innovation.

Our Products

That Powers Progress

Our commitment to quality and compliance is reflected in our globally recognized certifications: ISO 9001:2015 for quality management, ISO 14001:2015 for environmental responsibility, and IATF 16949:2016 for automotive-grade manufacturing. These standards are more than checkboxes, they define how we work, think, and deliver.

SPEN proudly serves some of the world's most demanding industries, including automotive, aerospace, hard disk drives, cameras, medical devices, and electronics. Whether you're launching a new product or seeking a long-term manufacturing partner, SPEN delivers the precision, reliability, and responsiveness your business demands.

Our Core Services

Quality
& Precision

At SPEN, precision isn’t a goal—it’s a guarantee. With machining tolerances under 3 microns and ISO 9001, IATF 16949, and ISO 14001 certifications, we ensure every part meets the highest standards.

Capabilities
& Equipment

With over 140 advanced CNC machines across two BOI-approved facilities, SPEN delivers high precision turning, milling, and complex component manufacturing at scale.

Process Control

Management System Certified

All products are inspected for quality throughout the production process by the qualified inspector. All high quality raw materials are inspected on arrival. Inspection continues throughout the production process, followed by random testing of products, using accurate and high technology calibration instruments and equipment with management according to international quality and environmental standards.

Single Point Engineering Company Limited commits to environmental management by adhering to the requirements and related law in order to use resources worthily, including reduce water pollution, noise pollution, air pollution, soil contamination and various wastes, as well as continual improvement and development to maintain the good quality of life for employees and communities.
